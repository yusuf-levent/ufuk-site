What Ufuk does

AI coding agent

Plan, edit, refactor and debug with an agent that understands your whole workspace — not just the open file.

Multiple AI models

Pick the right model for each task, from fast everyday helpers to heavyweight reasoning models. Switch any time.

Credit-based pricing

Every plan includes a monthly credit pool. Credits are shown live in the app, so there are no surprise bills.

Built on VS Code

A familiar, keyboard-first desktop editor with extensions, themes and keybindings you already know.

English & Turkish

The interface ships in English by default with a Turkish language pack included.

Your code stays yours

Projects stay on your machine. See the Privacy page for exactly what is processed and why.

Simple pricing

Every plan is billed monthly and includes a monthly credit pool. Unused credits do not roll over. Prices in USD.
